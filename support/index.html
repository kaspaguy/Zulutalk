Support

Help with ZuluTalk comes from us, not from Linphone.

Report a problem

Email reports@zulutalk.com. Tell us your phone model, the ZuluTalk version (Help → About), what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

You can also open an issue on GitHub. Logs can contain phone numbers and SIP addresses, so please email them rather than attaching them to a public issue.

Sending logs

Version 1.2: please don't use Help → Troubleshooting → Share logs. In this version it uploads your logs to Linphone's server and addresses the email to Linphone's support team. Email reports@zulutalk.com with a description of the problem instead, and we will tell you how to send logs.

Version 1.3 and later: Share logs attaches your logs to an email to reports@zulutalk.com. Nothing is uploaded.

Belledonne Communications does not support ZuluTalk. Only report a problem to Linphone if it also happens in the original Linphone app.

Setting up an account

ZuluTalk needs a SIP account. Your provider gives you a username, password and server address, or a setup link that configures the app for you. ZuluTalk does not sell phone service or numbers.
